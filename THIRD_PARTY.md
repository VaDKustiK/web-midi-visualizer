# Third-party assets

This file consolidates the attribution and processing details for all bundled samples. Keep it with redistributed assets.

## Grand piano

`public/samples/C1.mp3` through `C7.mp3` are an unchanged subset of **Salamander Grand Piano** by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Original library and license: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
- MP3 conversion source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
- Playback transposes the nearest C recording. This is a compact single-velocity subset, not the complete library.
- Credit Alexander Holm when redistributing the samples or sharing piano exports. No endorsement is implied.

## Soft piano

Files: `public/samples/soft/C1.mp3` through `C7.mp3`.

Salamander Grand Piano by **Alexander Holm**, licensed under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano/tree/3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404/Samples

These are C1–C7 recordings from velocity layer 3 (`C1v3.flac` through `C7v3.flac`), separate from the grand-piano MP3s. Prepared with FFmpeg: peak gain adjusted to -3 dB, limited to 12 seconds with a final half-second fade where applicable, resampled to 44.1 kHz, and encoded as MP3 quality 3. Runtime playback adds a gentle low-pass filter and 18 ms attack.

This is a compact single-velocity subset, not the complete Salamander instrument. Credit Alexander Holm when redistributing the samples or sharing sampled-piano exports. No endorsement is implied.

## Acoustic guitar

Files: `public/samples/guitar/`.

Acoustic guitar recordings from the **University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios**, edited and distributed by **N. P. Brosowsky** in **tonejs-instruments**, under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Source: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/tree/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/samples/guitar-acoustic
- Source attribution: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/sample-source-info.txt
- Sample license: https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/blob/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5/README.md#license

Nine MP3 files are included unchanged: E2, A2, C3, E3, G3, C4, E4, G4, B4. Playback transposes the nearest recording and applies a short release envelope. No additional distortion or reverb is applied. Credit the source and editor when redistributing the samples or sharing guitar exports. No endorsement is implied.

## Generated instruments

Electric keys and Synth are generated by the project's Web Audio code and contain no third-party instrument recordings.

## Fonts

DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono are bundled locally through `@fontsource` packages under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their license files are included in the package distributions. No Google Fonts runtime request is made.

## Icons and dependencies

Lucide icons use the ISC license. React, Vite, @tonejs/midi, Mediabunny and gifenc retain their respective upstream licenses. Exact versions are recorded in `package-lock.json`.

The demo MIDI at `public/midi/demo.mid` is authored by **flojy** and supplied separately. Music rights remain with flojy unless separately specified; the project's MIT code license does not automatically apply to the demo music. The generated notes used as gallery placeholders when this file is absent are not flojy's composition and are never played by the demo button.
